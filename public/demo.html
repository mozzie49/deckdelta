What to look for

  1. Load the included fictional Aster Studio PDFs with one click.
  2. Slide 4 → 2 has both Moved and Changed badges. The bars changed even though the extracted text did not.
  3. Slide 3 → 3 shows the removed $39 and added $49 numeric tokens.
  4. The two identical appendix pages are marked Check match. The warning explains the ambiguity.
  5. Open Correct this match to see the manual pairing controls. The recording does not choose an arbitrary pairing for identical pages.
